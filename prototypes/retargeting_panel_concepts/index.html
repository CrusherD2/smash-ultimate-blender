Retargeting: three concepts

Every control in today's panel is kept: Guided Mode, Bake Actions, Bind To + Bind Armatures, bind settings + Apply Bind Settings, presets (+ / −), Map by Proximity + Match Radius, Custom Bones, Core / Arms / Legs / Fingers / Root slots with eyedroppers and mirroring, twist bones, Throw, Deform Preset, Clear All, and the Actions tools. The concepts change order, labels and density, not what the tools do. Sample: mapping a Mixamo rig onto Mario.

A. Follow the workflow, top to bottom

Today Bind sits above Mapping and Bake sits at the very top, though you map first and bake last. Numbered steps put things in working order and show what's done. Guided Mode becomes "Next step" on the current one.
